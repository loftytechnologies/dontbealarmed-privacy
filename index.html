Don't be alarmed — Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

"Don't be alarmed" is an alarm clock app that wakes you at one time if the weather forecast for that morning is dry, and at a later time if it is raining. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version

We don't run any servers, don't have accounts, don't show ads, and don't collect or sell your personal information. Your alarms and your chosen town stay on your phone. The only thing that leaves your phone is the town you search for and that town's coordinates, which are sent to a free weather service to get the forecast.

Information stored on your device

  • Your alarms: times, repeat days, and on/off settings.
  • The Australian town or suburb you choose, with its approximate coordinates.

This is kept only in the app's storage on your phone. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Information sent to third parties

To work out the weather, the app contacts Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a free weather data service:

  • Place search: the text you type into the location box is sent to find matching Australian towns.
  • Forecast: the approximate coordinates of your chosen town are sent to get the weather forecast for your alarm times.

These requests are made over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Like any internet service, Open-Meteo receives your device's IP address when the request is made. The app does not use your phone's GPS, and does not ask for location permission. Open-Meteo's own policies apply to how it handles requests.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to sound your alarm at the right time.
  • Exact alarms: so the alarm goes off at the exact minute you set rather than a few minutes late.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
